Australia has one of the most expensive property markets in the world, particularly in Sydney and Melbourne. Understanding how your home loan repayments are calculated is essential before committing to a mortgage.

## How Australian Home Loans Work

In Australia, mortgages are called home loans. Most lenders offer principal and interest loans (where you pay down the balance each month) or interest-only loans (common for investors). Owner-occupiers almost always choose principal and interest.

Australian lenders calculate repayments monthly, fortnightly, or weekly. Choosing fortnightly repayments instead of monthly is one of the most effective ways to pay off your loan faster.

## Real Example

Property price: $750,000. Deposit: 20% ($150,000). Loan amount: $600,000. Interest rate: 6.0%. Term: 30 years. Monthly repayment: approximately $3,597. Fortnightly repayment: approximately $1,660.

## Why Fortnightly Payments Save Money

Paying fortnightly means you make 26 half-payments per year, which equals 13 full monthly payments instead of 12. That one extra payment per year reduces a 30-year loan term by approximately 4 years and saves tens of thousands in interest.

## Loan to Value Ratio (LVR)

LVR is the percentage of the property value you are borrowing. A $600,000 loan on a $750,000 property is an 80% LVR. Lenders require Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI) if your LVR exceeds 80%, which adds thousands to your upfront costs. Saving a 20% deposit avoids LMI entirely.

## Stamp Duty in Australia

Each state charges stamp duty (also called transfer duty) on property purchases. The amount varies by state and property value. First home buyers receive concessions or exemptions in most states. In NSW, stamp duty on a $750,000 property is approximately $29,000 for non-first-home buyers.

## The Reserve Bank of Australia and Interest Rates

The RBA sets the official cash rate which influences variable home loan rates across all lenders. When the RBA raises rates, your variable rate mortgage repayment increases. Many Australians fix their rate for 1 to 3 years to get certainty over repayments.
