Paying off your mortgage early is one of the best financial moves you can make. Even small extra payments can save tens of thousands of dollars in interest and free you from debt years ahead of schedule.

## Strategy 1 — Make Extra Monthly Payments

Adding even $100 to $200 per month to your principal payment has a dramatic effect over time. On a $320,000 loan at 6.5% over 30 years, an extra $200 per month saves over $60,000 in interest and cuts 4.5 years off your loan term.

The key is to specify that the extra payment goes toward principal, not future payments. Contact your lender or note it clearly in your payment.

## Strategy 2 — Switch to Biweekly Payments

Instead of making 12 monthly payments per year, make 26 biweekly half-payments. This results in one extra full payment per year. On a 30-year mortgage, this simple change alone cuts approximately 4 years off your loan and saves thousands in interest.

## Strategy 3 — Make One Extra Payment Per Year

If biweekly payments are complicated to set up, simply make one extra lump sum payment per year equal to one monthly payment. Apply it entirely to principal. The effect is nearly identical to the biweekly strategy.

## Strategy 4 — Refinance to a Shorter Term

Refinancing from a 30-year to a 15-year mortgage increases your monthly payment but dramatically reduces your total interest paid. A $320,000 loan at 6.5% over 30 years costs $408,000 in total interest. The same loan over 15 years at 6.0% costs $166,000 in total interest — a saving of over $240,000.

## Strategy 5 — Apply Windfalls to Principal

Tax refunds, bonuses, and inheritances applied directly to your mortgage principal can shave years off your loan. A single $5,000 lump sum payment in year 5 of a 30-year mortgage can save over $15,000 in future interest.
